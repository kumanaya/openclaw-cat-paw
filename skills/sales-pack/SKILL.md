# Sales pack

Two trees. Open one playbook. Do not send the email yourself. A draft
the owner approves is the work. Sending from the agent's line is
`plow-chat`. Sending from the owner's mailbox is Latch, and only after
they say yes.

## SDR — `skills/marketing/`

[coreyhaines31/marketingskills](https://github.com/coreyhaines31/marketingskills)
at `5b2c0007`. MIT.

| Ask | Playbook |
| --- | --- |
| First note to a stranger | `cold-email` |
| Who to talk to, and why them | `prospecting` |
| Leave the account executive something they can use | `sales-enablement` |

## Sales engineer — `skills/sales/sales-engineer/`

[alirezarezvani/claude-skills](https://github.com/alirezarezvani/claude-skills)
at `19392f7a`. MIT. Demo script and POC scorecard ship inside that
folder. Follow them. A live demo on the owner's machine is Latch.

A renewal, a QBR, or a churn save is `customer-success-pack`.
A pricing page is `marketing-pack`. A PRD is `product-pack`.
