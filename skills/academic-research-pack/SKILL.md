[Imbad0202/academic-research-skills](https://github.com/Imbad0202/academic-research-skills)

with attribution. `LICENSE`, `NOTICE.md`, and `CITATION.cff` are copied
beside the playbooks. Do not strip them. Do not restate this pack as MIT.

Playbooks: `skills/academic-research/<name>/SKILL.md`

| Ask | Playbook |
| --- | --- |
| Write or revise a paper | `academic-paper` |
| Review a paper | `academic-paper-reviewer` |
| The whole loop, from question to final | `academic-pipeline` |
| A literature search with sources | `deep-research` |

Open that `SKILL.md` and follow it, including its references and
templates. Do not invent a citation style. Do not claim a source you
did not open.

This is scholarship, not a security engagement. A host, a CVE, or a
scan is `cybersecurity-pack`. A product PRD is `product-pack`.

If `skills/academic-research/` is missing, tell the owner to run
`scripts/install-skills.sh` (Windows: `install-skills.ps1`). The four
names above are not a substitute for the playbook.
